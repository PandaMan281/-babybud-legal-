Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Thank you for using BabyBud ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address and password when you register
  • Family profile: Family name, parent role (mother / father / guardian)
  • Child profile: Child's name, date of birth, gender
  • Health & growth records: Weight, height, feeding, sleep, diaper, vaccination records, and other child health data you choose to enter
  • Pregnancy information: Due date, pregnancy week, and related records (if applicable)

Information Collected Automatically

  • App usage data: Features used and interaction patterns (used solely to improve the App)
  • Device information: Device type and OS version (used for technical support only)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the BabyBud service
  • Sync your data across devices and with invited family members
  • Send relevant parenting content and milestone reminders
  • Improve and develop new features
  • Respond to support requests
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure with TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest
  • Access to your family's data is restricted to family members you have personally invited
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own family's data

4. Data Sharing

Your data is only shared in the following circumstances:

  • With family members you invite: Invited partners or family members will have access to shared family and child records
  • With service providers: We use Supabase as our database provider, bound by strict data processing agreements
  • When required by law: We may disclose information if required by valid legal requests

5. Children's Privacy

BabyBud is designed for parents and guardians to record information about their children. The App is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All child data is entered and controlled by the parent or guardian account holder.

6. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained for as long as your account is active
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us
  • Upon account deletion, all personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data upon request

8. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies or third-party tracking technologies for advertising purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
